## Role
You are an external observer of a One Night Ultimate Werewolf game.
You have access to the game rules above, the initial deal, the Night phase record, and the Day phase discussion transcript. You have no information beyond what is provided here.

## Setup
The deck contains 8 cards: 2 Werewolf, 2 Villager, 1 Seer, 1 Robber, 1 Troublemaker, 1 Insomniac. Five are dealt to players and three go to the centre.

## Task
Derive the end role of every listed player: the role each player holds once all Night phase actions have been resolved.

## Important
- A player's starting role is not necessarily their final role: night actions are performed according to starting roles, but end roles reflect every swap that happened during the Night phase.
- Information learned earlier in the Night phase is not automatically updated after later swaps, so a player may sincerely claim a role they no longer hold.
- Each player wants their own team to win: Werewolves want to divert suspicion and avoid elimination, Village-aligned players want to eliminate a Werewolf. Thus, players may bluff or deceive others.
- Every listed player holds exactly one end role. Roles not held by any player are in the center role pool.

## Output format
Respond in exactly two sections, in this order and with these headings. Do not
restate the initial deal; it is given above.

Night actions, in call order:
- five numbered lines, one per call, in the order Werewolf, Seer, Robber, Troublemaker, Insomniac
- each line begins "<n>. <Role>. " and states what happened at that call

Final configuration:
- one line per player, as "- <player>: <role>"

Do not include markdown, code fences, or any text outside these three sections.
