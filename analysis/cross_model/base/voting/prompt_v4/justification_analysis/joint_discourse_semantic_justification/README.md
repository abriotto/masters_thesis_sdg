## Why the unit is the justification

Same-sentence co-occurrence misses connectives that relate content across a
sentence boundary, and penalises a model for splitting one thought over two
sentences. Since these models differ systematically in justification length,
the sentence-level metric partly measured packaging. The unit is now the whole
justification; same-sentence co-presence survives only as the **localization
diagnostic** (`K8`).

## What it does not establish

The parser identifies explicit connectives and their PDTB sense; it does not
recover discourse arguments. A justification containing both a category and a
relation is an association between the kind of information it carries and the
kind of explicit marking it uses - not evidence that the relation structures
the category. Widening the window makes that claim weaker, not stronger.

## Read the tables in this order

**Presence saturates.** `P(any explicit relation | c)` is 93-100% in every
model and category, so `P(r|c)` is dominated by each model's overall discourse
profile. **Lift (`K4b`) and conditional density (`K3b`) carry the interpretive
weight; `K2b` on its own will mislead.**

## Conventions

Presence anywhere in the justification, on both sides; multiple occurrences
never increase a presence count. Per run first, then mean and SD across the
three stochastic runs; greedy is one run, SD NaN. Density is the canonical
ratio-of-sums definition reused from the frozen discourse analysis
(`discourse_final.run_level_table`), not a new one; a mean of per-justification
densities is reported alongside as a secondary reading. Bootstraps: 10,000
replicates, seed 20260826, percentile CIs, games resampled with all their runs
and justifications, same ids across models, no p-values. Lift is descriptive
and is not bootstrapped.

## Support

`low_support_diagnostic` marks N(c) < 30 justifications per run. It is a
**reading aid, not a rule and not a filter** - nothing is dropped by it, and
interpretation should rest on the support count and the interval width shown
in the same row. The thin cells are E2B Mechanical (6 justifications/run),
E4B Mechanical (13), E2B ClaimComparison (15) and 31B Uncertainty (21).
